Ratchet & Clank

In a galaxy full of danger and adventure, Ratchet, a daring mechanical Lombax, and Clank, his inseparable android companion, embark on an epic mission to save their worlds from an intergalactic threat. Armed with outlandish weapons and unique abilities, they explore diverse planets and face unpredictable challenges, uncovering secrets that will change their destiny.

Release: 2002

Demon's Souls

In the dark kingdom of Boletaria, an ancestral curse unleashes demonic hordes that threaten to consume the world. The heroes must confront terrible creatures and traverse desolate landscapes to unravel the mysteries of darkness and restore the lost balance.

Resident Evil Village

In the mysterious and foreboding world of Resident Evil Village, Ethan Winters finds himself entangled in a desperate fight for survival against grotesque creatures and sinister forces. Tasked with rescuing his daughter, he must navigate a haunting village filled with danger at every turn, uncovering dark secrets and unraveling a complex web of horror.

Release: 2021
